This is the demo blog for `astro-awesomeness`. If this page renders with the
theme's typography, header, footer, theme toggle, and tag chips, the primitives
are wired up correctly.

## What you should see

- A sticky header with the site title and a dark-mode toggle.
- Body text in the sans-serif you chose (`Inter Variable` for the demo).
- Tag chips at the top.
- A footer at the bottom.

## What you should _not_ see

- A flash of unstyled content (`base-layout.astro` inlines the theme init).
- Layout shift from the theme toggle.
